Please read: prototype notice

LUXMY is an experimental student prototype. It is not a medical device. Please do not rely on it for real medication reminders. All medicines shown here are fictional demonstrations, and LUXMY does not give medical advice. For any question about your medicines, please ask a doctor or pharmacist.

Reminders can only sound while this page is open and active in your browser. They will not work if the browser is closed, the tab is asleep, or the phone is locked.
